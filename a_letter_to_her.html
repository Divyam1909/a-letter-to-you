Breaking the Curse

You know how we meet some people and simply forget, Then there are those you meet and somehow never regret, I never expected a stranger I met to become this close to me, Yet somehow, in barely two months, you became the person I long to see.

I still remember those first conversations we shared, Little pieces of our lives, quietly revealed and compared, I never knew that somewhere between a hello and goodnight, A certain girl would slowly make my ordinary world feel right.

You arrived without warning, without some grand design, And somehow your little world began becoming part of mine, What started with messages became something I could feel, A connection so effortless, I sometimes wonder if it’s the real deal.

They say beautiful things are meant to be seen, In paintings, in sunsets, and in places untouched and serene, But now beauty, to me, is no longer somewhere far away, It has your eyes, your smile, and the little things that you say.

Your eyes have a way of making silence feel complete, Like every restless thought in me suddenly finds its seat, And when you smile, there is something I cannot explain, Like sunlight somehow learning to fall as a gentle rain.

You have this way of thinking about the smallest detail, Turning every tiny thought into a story without fail, Sometimes you overthink what the moment might mean, Yet somehow that thoughtful heart is one of the sweetest things I’ve seen.

You know, you are a little dicey, and your choices are grey, And I might not always know what you want to say, But perhaps that is one reason your heart feels so rare, Because you never choose carelessly, you choose because you care.

My fav part is when you sing, and the whole world seems to slow, As though even the quietest corners want to hear your glow, If the heavens ever needed a voice to sing beneath its blue, I think it would want to borrow a little melody from you.

There is something beautiful in the kindness that you show, The way your softer side appears without needing me to know, You make room for little lives, for every creature and every hue, And somehow that gentle part of you makes me admire you more than I knew.

Perhaps flowers remind me of you for reasons I cannot explain, For every bloom holds a beauty, but yours feels different all the same, Carnations, tulips, lilies, each can brighten any view, But if beauty had a name, I think it would sound like you.

They call the carnation the flower of the gods, adorned in sacred grace, And I can almost understand it when I look upon your face, Its petals hold a softness, its colours warm and bright, Yet somehow you carry all of that, with much more in sight.

Yellow like the sunlight dancing softly through the trees, Pink like a feeling carried gently on the breeze, Green like the peaceful little world I find when I’m with you, Perhaps that is why these colours feel so perfectly true.

Maybe it’s momos on a street beneath the evening glow, Or pani puri while we laugh and have nowhere else to go, Maybe pizza, maybe pasta, maybe something entirely new, Truth is, I’d probably enjoy it all, as long as I’m there with you.

There is something about the songs you love that stays in my head, Like little pieces of you hiding inside every word that is said, From Strawberries and Cigarettes to Heavenly Kind of State of Mind, Each one somehow leaves another little picture of you behind.

You love Spider-Man, the one who keeps reaching for the sky, The one who falls, gets back up, and still chooses to try, Perhaps that is why it feels fitting, somehow, to see you that way, A little extraordinary soul making ordinary moments brighter every day.

And now there have been two dates, with another waiting somewhere ahead, Another little chapter that neither of us has completely read, Yet somehow I already find myself hoping, quietly, that there is more, Because every time I leave you, I find myself wanting another door.

I call you S because one letter somehow says your name, And princess sometimes, because honestly, you earned that frame, You call me honey, and I pretend that it does not make me smile, But I keep those little words with me long after every mile.

Maybe love is not always lightning, dramatic and bright, Maybe sometimes it is simply feeling at home on an ordinary night, Maybe it is laughing at nothing until the hours disappear, And slowly realizing there is one person you always want near.

I wish the distance between us were only a little less, So seeing you could happen without waiting, without a guess, For when a week or two passes before I see you again, I find myself wishing you lived just a little closer than you do.

Maybe it is knowing someone’s chaos and still choosing to stay, Knowing every little habit and loving them anyway, Knowing she may overthink, hesitate, sleep late, and take her time, And still thinking every imperfect part somehow fits perfectly with mine.

So I have compared you to flowers, to sunsets, to the sea, To stars and songs and every beautiful thing I could see, But every metaphor eventually feels too small to hold true, Because the more I try to describe beauty, the more it leads me to you.

So before I compare you to another star in the night, Before I call you my moon or my sunrise light, Let me leave the universe behind and simply speak from my heart, Because perhaps the most beautiful things are the ones we never know how to start.

Could I tell you that somewhere between every laugh and every call, A little part of me began to hope that you might become my all? Not because I need some perfect story written in the stars above, I simply found myself falling, quietly and honestly, in love.

Because every road I take somehow circles back to you, Every little future I imagine feels warmer when you are in view, You have become a thought that visits me before I sleep, One I find myself holding onto, one I find myself wanting to keep.

Under all the teasing, the coffee, the songs, the dates we have made, Rests one simple feeling that time has only deepened, never swayed, Beyond every flower, every colour, every beautiful view, One truth keeps growing quietly: I really, really like you.

You are the girl I want beside me when ordinary days unfold, For all the little stories we have yet to write and memories yet to hold, Rather than promise perfection, I would promise to choose you true, In every silly, beautiful, complicated moment we stumble through.

Every word before this was simply my heart finding its way, Now there is only one thing I have been wanting to say, Do you think there’s a chance our story could become something new? The future is unknown, but could I make a little forever with you?

- D.N.
